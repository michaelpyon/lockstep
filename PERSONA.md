# PERSONA.md - Lockstep

## The person

**"Dani", 27, QA analyst in Columbus, Ohio.** Grew up on a DS Lite playing Rhythm Heaven (the "Lockstep" minigame specifically broke her brain in the best way). Owns a Switch but it lives at home; her workday runs on a locked-down Windows laptop where the only game console available is a Chrome tab. She keeps a mechanical keyboard at her desk and treats the spacebar like an instrument.

She is 1 specific person, not "rhythm gamers": the DS-era Rhythm Heaven nostalgic who never migrated to osu! because osu! is a lifestyle, and she wants a toy, not a grind.

## The job she hires Lockstep for

"Give me a 3 minute rhythm toy I can open in a browser tab between meetings that actually tests my internal clock, and give me a number I can rub in my friends' faces." The job is a coffee-break skill check with a bragging artifact at the end. Not practice, not progression, not an account. Open tab, march, screenshot, close tab.

## Where she hangs out online

- **r/rhythmgames** (lurks, upvotes clips, rarely posts)
- **The Rhythm Heaven fan Discord** (the big one linked from r/RhythmHeaven), specifically the #other-games and #scores style channels
- **X**, following @rhythmheaven fan accounts and indie web-game devs; retweets Wario Ware and RH remix clips
- Occasionally **itch.io** browser rhythm games when someone links one

## What she uses today instead

- Rhythm Heaven Megamix on a 3DS she has to dig out of a drawer
- osu! on her home PC (feels like homework)
- A Trombone Champ clip habit on X (watches more than plays)
- Random itch.io rhythm jams that usually have broken audio latency

## Taste: 3 named products she considers premium

1. **Rhythm Heaven Fever** - the gold standard: flat bold color fields, snap cuts, sound-first design, zero UI clutter during play
2. **Trombone Champ** - proof a dumb-looking browser-adjacent rhythm game can be shareable and beloved
3. **A Dance of Fire and Ice** - minimal geometry, brutal precision, instantly readable at a glance in a clip

## Her vocabulary

"On-beat / off-beat", "the switch", "SUPERB run", "full combo" (FC), "the timing window feels tight/loose", "input latency", "that cue is so readable", "clean". She says a game "feels crunchy" when hits have weight. She never says "gamify" or "engagement".

## Device

Windows work laptop, Chrome, external mechanical keyboard, wired or laptop speakers at low volume (sometimes cheap earbuds). 1080p external monitor. She will not play this on her phone and does not expect to.

## 5 second bounce triggers

1. Press Space and hear nothing (dead AudioContext) - instant close
2. Beat dot visibly drifting from the metronome tick - "latency, bye"
3. Wall of instructions before she can press anything - she learns by doing, like Rhythm Heaven's "try again until you feel it"
4. Generic system-font UI that looks like a coding tutorial output - reads as "this will feel bad too"
5. A share button that produces broken or scoreless text - kills the whole reason she came

## The unprompted share moment

She survives the rapid-switch section at the end, lands **SUPERB!** with the whole marcher grid in perfect sync, and the results screen hands her a 1-click line like "SUPERB! on Lockstep: 8420 pts, 14x combo. Beat my score: [link]". She screenshots the SUPERB screen (it must look like a poster, not a debug readout) and drops it in the Discord #scores channel with "ok this one is actually evil". The ?challenge= link turning her score into a dare for the next person is what makes it spread.

## Verdict on the V2 evangelist

The V2 card's persona (Rhythm Heaven fan, 20s-30s, r/rhythmgames lurker, no Switch at work, screenshots a SUPERB run) is correct and is kept, sharpened into Dani above. 1 correction: she is not an osu!/Beat Saber player looking for a substitute; she is a lapsed Rhythm Heaven player who finds those games too heavy. That distinction matters for tone - Lockstep should feel like a toy with teeth, not a lite version of a hardcore rhythm game.
